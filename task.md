Let's make another game.

Preferrable in browser, client-only mode, so that it can be run without a served.

THis is a game called "Choo-choo train".

The objective is to solve puzzles by matching items with a train and it's carriages.

Once the puzzle has been solved, the train leaves with sound and smoke effects.

In an elementary level, it could be as simple as sorting items in carriages.
For example, three items are shown above the train: letter A, number 3, and a pair of cherries. The carriages of the train are labeled with letter C, picture of a lemon, and number 6. The correct order is to match number with number, letter with letter, and fruit with fruit.

It can get progressively more complicated.

Feel free to use some IQ-test like puzzles as inspiration. Don't require too deep domain knownledge but rather pattern matching and ability to think logically.
